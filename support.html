Personal Trip Organizer Support

Personal Trip Organizer, displayed as Trip Organizer on your iPhone, helps you organize trips, expenses, plans, photos, and packing lists. It is free to use and requires no account.

Contact support

For help, bug reports, or feature requests, email montnord.beishan@gmail.com. No GitHub account is needed.

Include the app version and build shown on the welcome screen, your iPhone model, iOS version, what you expected, and the steps that led to the problem. Share only the minimum information needed. Do not send passwords, device passcodes, payment details, private trip records, or unredacted receipts and screenshots. The developer cannot access your locally stored trips.

Common questions

Do I need internet access or an account?

No. Core trip, manual expense, plan, packing, and search features work offline without signing in. Selecting a photo stored only in iCloud may require a system download. Sharing to an online service depends on the destination you choose.

How do I add an item?

Create or open a trip, select Expenses, Plans, Photos, or Packing, then tap +. The + action follows the selected tab. For an expense, choose Manual to enter it yourself.

Why is expense image scanning unavailable?

Image extraction requires an eligible iPhone running iOS 27 or later with Apple Intelligence enabled and its on-device model ready. English documents and responses are supported. You can always use manual expense entry instead. Review and correct extracted suggestions and confirm the currency before saving.

Why are currency estimates different from current rates?

Estimates use bundled reference rates, not live exchange rates, and some currencies have no estimate. Original amounts are preserved. Enter a manual conversion when needed.

How do I unlock the app?

Optional App Lock uses your iPhone's Face ID, Touch ID, or device passcode. There is no separate app password or developer reset code. Support cannot bypass device authentication.

Where are my trips, and how do I back them up?

Trips and imported images are stored locally, with no app-managed cloud synchronization. They are eligible for normal iOS device backups, depending on your settings. Verify a current backup before deleting the app. Do not uninstall the app as a troubleshooting step: uninstalling removes its local data. Whole-trip export and archive import are not available.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual items or use Trip Options > Delete Trip to remove a trip and its local attachments. Copies you previously shared and earlier device backups are managed separately.
